I am an active sailor in the DMV region, where I own a Beneteau 331 named "O Fabjuous Day" and sail out of Deale, MD. I am a member of the Sailing Club of Washington, and the Herrington Harbor Sailing Association. As my interest in sailing has increased, I've started to try and compile resources for others interested in sailing, and as a repository for my own interests and travels.
