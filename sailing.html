I am an active sailor in the DMV region, where I own a Beneteau 331 named "O Fabulous Day" and sail out of Deale, MD. I am a member of the Sailing Club of Washington and the Herrington Harbour Sailing Association. As my interest in sailing has increased, I've started to compile resources for others interested in sailing, and as a repository for my own interests and travels.

Featured Project: Beneteau 331 Sailboat Docking & Slip Maneuvering Simulator
A realistic, browser-based 2D physics simulation built to practice tight marina slip maneuvers. Features authentic single-screw prop walk (reverse stern walk to port), prop wash, engine throttle, wind drift, tidal currents, and pilings/spring lines.
