Skipper's Docking Guide: Beneteau 331

1. Single-Screw Handling & Beta 25 Engine

  • Prop Wash in Forward: In forward gear, the propeller discharges a high-velocity stream of water directly past the spade rudder. You can steer or "kick the stern" by giving a brief pulse of forward throttle with the wheel turned, even with zero forward boat speed!
  • Prop Walk in Reverse: When in reverse, the right-hand propeller acts like a paddlewheel against the hull, pulling the stern firmly to PORT. This means when backing into or out of the slip, your bow swings starboard. Steerage in reverse only takes effect once the boat gains sternway through the water.
  • Neutral Steerage: In neutral, the rudder blade only works if water is moving past the hull (Speed Through Water). As soon as the boat stops, rudder authority vanishes.

2. Slip Geometry & The Short Port Finger Pier

  • 1/3 Finger Pier: The finger pier on your port side extends only about one-third of the boat length (~11.5 ft / 3.5 m). Your bow and midship are supported, but the aft two-thirds of the boat overhangs into the fairway past the finger pier!
  • The Four Pilings: Two outer pilings mark the slip mouth, and two inner pilings anchor the head dock. Outer pilings are ideal for stern lines to prevent the stern from swinging out.
  • Spring Line Snubbing: In crosswinds or when docking solo, running a line from your Port Midship Spring Cleat to the Finger Pier End Cleat and motoring gently forward with the wheel turned away from the dock will snug the boat perfectly against the pier fenders.

3. Wind, Current & Leeway Dynamics

  • Bow Blow-Off: Sailboats have high bow freeboard and low cockpits. When drifting at low speed, the wind blows the bow downwind much faster than the stern. Always maintain just enough headway to maintain steerage until your spring or bow line is attached.
  • Tidal Current: Current pushes the entire water column. Watch the Course Over Ground (COG) vs Heading (HDG) to compensate with crab angle.

  • S or ▼: Shift Neutral / Reverse (Step Down Detents: Forward $\rightarrow$ Neutral $\rightarrow$ Reverse Walk $\rightarrow$ Full Reverse)
  • N: Instant Neutral Detent (0%)
  • Space: Instant Center Wheel (0°)
  • A / D or ◀ / ▶: Wheel Turn Port / Starboard

  • Mouse Click: Click boat cleat & dock piling to tie line
